# 🏠 Roofcraft — High-Conversion Roofing Lead-Generation System

> **Project Vision:** A high-performance, conversion-focused digital sales tool engineered specifically for US-based home-service contractors (Primary Target Metro: Austin, TX). Built to replace traditional static brochure websites with an active, high-trust lead generation engine.

---

## 🎯 Executive Vision & Homeowner Psychology

Homeowners seeking roof repairs or full replacements are preparing for high-stakes financial decisions (often ranging from $10,000 to $30,000+). They do not visit a roofing website to admire fancy animations or complex interactive gimmicks—they visit to answer five core questions within seconds:

1. **Can I trust this company?** (Are they local, licensed, insured, and verified by real customer reviews?)
2. **Do they do the exact work I need?** (Residential replacements, emergency repairs, storm damage, gutters, commercial roofs.)
3. **What does their work actually look like?** (Real project photography and local before/after results.)
4. **What is the process and cost?** (Clear 4-step execution plan, transparent estimates, and available financing.)
5. **How quickly can I get an estimate?** (Low-friction contact paths and immediate telephone availability.)

Our site architecture is designed to systematically remove every point of homeowner hesitation, converting passive web traffic into high-intent estimate requests.

## 📸 Reference Blueprint Analysis & Antigravity Instructions

### Directives for Antigravity Implementation

When building out the visual UI and page flow based on our reference concept (`roofcraft-design-reference.png`), strictly adhere to the following design directives:

### 1. Hero Section Strategy (No Gimmicks)
* **What to Exclude:** Do NOT generate interactive "AI Roof Style Generators" or upload widgets in the hero. Real homeowners in urgent need of roofing services view these as gimmicks rather than trust signals.
* **What to Include:** Focus the hero area strictly on **high conversion and instant proof**:
  * Strong value proposition headline: *"Built to Protect What Matters. Austin’s Trusted Roofing & Exterior Experts."*
  * High-contrast badge confirming **Licensed & Insured** status and **Manufacturer Certifications**.
  * Primary Action: High-visibility `[ Request Free Estimate ]` CTA button paired with a direct `[ Call (512) 555-0198 ]` action.
  * Verified Google Review Overlay (4.9★ rating based on 340+ customer reviews).

### 2. Immediate Social Proof & Authority Strip
* Position a dedicated metric strip directly beneath the hero section to immediately establish market presence:
  * **15+ Years** Experience
  * **1,250+** Projects Completed
  * **4.9 / 5.0★** Average Customer Rating
  * **100%** Licensed & Insured

### 3. Proof-First Portfolio Showcase
* Feature real-world project cards displaying local geotags (*e.g., Shingle Replacement — Austin, TX*) and before/after comparisons so potential clients can verify local craftsmanship.

### 4. Process Transparency & Price Friction Removal
* Dedicate clear sections to the **4-Step Homeowner Journey** (*Request ➔ Inspect ➔ Estimate ➔ Build*) and flexible payment/financing options to address pricing anxiety directly.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack & Tooling

To ensure top-tier performance, instantaneous load times, excellent mobile responsiveness, and easy client handover, the application will use the following tech stack:

* **Core Library:** [React](https://react.dev/) — Component-driven, modular UI architecture for high reusability across future client demos.
* **Language:** [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/) — Strict type safety for all component interfaces, site configuration data, and lead form state handling.
* **Styling Framework:** [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) — Utility-first, responsive design framework for precise spacing, modern typography, and fast rendering.
* **Iconography:** [Lucide React](https://lucide.dev/) — Crisp, lightweight vector icons representing trust signals, phone actions, and service categories.
* **Form Submission Backend:** External Serverless API (Web3Forms or Formspree) — Allows lead forms to send instant email alerts directly to contractors without requiring a complex backend database.
* **Build Partner:** **Antigravity** — AI-assisted execution engine generating clean, human-inspectable code under explicit developer oversight.
